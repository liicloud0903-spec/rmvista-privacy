# RMVista Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-10

RMVista (the "app", 书影音 in Chinese) is a reader, music player and video player that works with files already on your phone. It is made by an individual developer. Contact: liicloud0903@gmail.com

## Summary
- The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
- Your books, music, videos, reading progress, bookmarks and statistics stay on your device. The developer cannot see them.
- The app does not upload your content to any server.

## What the app stores on your device
Imported novels, bookmarks, reading progress, playlists, playback history, statistics and your settings are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app removes them. If you use "Export backup", the backup file is saved where you choose; keeping it safe is up to you, and it is not encrypted.

## Permissions
- **Music and audio / Photos and videos** (Android): only to find and play media you choose. Video folders are chosen through the system folder picker.
- **Files and folders**: only the folders you pick; the app does not browse other locations.
- **Internet / local network**: used by the optional "Wi-Fi transfer" feature, which starts a temporary web page reachable by devices on the same Wi-Fi so you can send books from a computer. It runs only while you turn it on, and nothing is sent to the developer. The internet permission is also used by Google Play for purchases.
- **Foreground service, wake lock**: to keep music playing in the background and the screen on while you watch.

## Purchases
The optional one-time "Pro" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. The app never sees your card or payment details. Google's own privacy policy applies to purchases.

## Dictionary and other data
Dictionary lookups use data bundled inside the app and work offline.

## Children
The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any personal information.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated date.

## Contact
liicloud0903@gmail.com

# RMVista（书影音）隐私政策

最后更新：2026-11-10

RMVista（中文名"书影音"）是一款读小说、听音乐、看视频的应用，处理的是你手机里已有的文件，由个人开发者制作。联系邮箱：liicloud0903@gmail.com

## 摘要
- 没有账号、没有广告、没有统计分析、没有跟踪。
- 你的书、音乐、视频、阅读进度、书签和统计数据都只保存在你的设备上，开发者看不到。
- 应用不会把你的内容上传到任何服务器。

## 保存在设备上的数据
导入的小说、书签、阅读进度、歌单、播放记录、统计和设置，都保存在应用自己的私有存储里，卸载应用即删除。使用"导出备份"时，备份文件保存在你选择的位置，由你自己保管，文件没有加密。

## 权限说明
- **音乐和音频 / 照片和视频**（Android）：只用于找到并播放你选择的媒体。视频文件夹通过系统的文件夹选择器授权。
- **文件和文件夹**：只访问你选择的文件夹，不会浏览其他位置。
- **网络 / 局域网**：用于可选的"WiFi 传书"功能：它会临时开启一个网页，同一 WiFi 下的电脑可以访问，用来把书传到手机。只在你打开期间运行，不会向开发者发送任何内容。网络权限同时用于 Google Play 的购买。
- **前台服务、保持唤醒**：用于音乐后台播放，以及看视频时保持亮屏。

## 购买
可选的一次性"Pro"买断完全由 Google Play 处理，应用看不到你的银行卡或支付信息。购买适用 Google 自己的隐私政策。

## 词典等数据
查词使用应用内置的数据，离线可用。

## 儿童
本应用不面向儿童，也不会有意收集任何个人信息。

## 政策变更
政策如有变更，新版本会发布在本网址，并更新日期。
